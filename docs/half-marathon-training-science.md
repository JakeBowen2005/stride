# Half Marathon Training Science & Workout Library

> Research-backed training guide for half marathon plan generation.
> Sources: Pfitzinger (Faster Road Racing), Daniels' Running Formula, Running Writings,
> Laura Norris Running, Runners Connect, PubMed physiological studies.

## Physiological Demands of the Half Marathon

- Half marathon is raced at **79 ± 5% of VO2max** (PubMed research on recreational runners)
- Race pace sits **at or slightly above lactate threshold** for most trained runners
- ~98-99% aerobic, ~1-2% anaerobic contribution
- Race duration: 1:10-2:30 for trained runners — long enough that fueling matters but short enough that glycogen depletion is rarely the limiter
- Key limiters: **Lactate threshold** (primary), VO2max (ceiling), running economy, muscular endurance
- The half marathon is the "threshold race" — the pace you can sustain for ~60-90 minutes maps closely to LT
- Requires the speed of a 10K runner AND the endurance of a marathoner — it's a hybrid event

## How the Half Marathon Sits Between 10K and Marathon

| Aspect | 10K | Half Marathon | Marathon |
|--------|-----|---------------|---------|
| Race intensity | 90-95% VO2max | ~79% VO2max | ~75% VO2max |
| Primary limiter | LT | LT + endurance | Glycogen + LT |
| Key workout | Threshold reps | Threshold + HMP long runs | MP long runs |
| Long run purpose | Aerobic base | Aerobic + race simulation | Race simulation |
| Fueling concern | None | Minor | Critical |
| Taper length | 10-14 days | 10-14 days | 2-3 weeks |
| Mileage needs | 40-70 mpw | 45-70 mpw | 50-80+ mpw |
| Race duration | 30-55 min | 70-150 min | 2:30-5:00+ |

## Key Training Systems for Half Marathon

### 1. Lactate Threshold Development (The #1 Priority)
- **Why**: Half marathon pace ≈ lactate threshold pace for most runners. Raising LT = faster HM.
- **Threshold pace**: ~15K to HM race pace (very close to HM goal pace for faster runners)
- **Pfitzinger's approach**: In the final 6 weeks, 2 LT workouts per week make sense
- **Steady-state runs**: Continuous runs at a pace slightly slower than LT — "especially beneficial for the half marathon"
- **Tempo progression**: Start with 20min continuous → build to 40min continuous or 3x2mi reps
- **Cruise intervals**: 5-15min reps at threshold with 1-3min rest — effective and sustainable
- **Research**: 6 weeks of 2 threshold sessions/week improved time to exhaustion at LT by ~50%

### 2. Half Marathon Pace (HMP) Specificity
- **Why**: You need to sustain HMP for 70-120+ minutes. Must be ingrained.
- **How**: HMP segments in long runs (last 3-6 miles), continuous HMP runs (4-8 miles)
- **Key principle**: HMP sits between threshold and marathon pace — it's its own specific effort
- **Long run integration**: The long run becomes a workout in the final 6-8 weeks (HMP finish)
- **Progression**: Start with 2mi at HMP in long runs → 3mi → 4mi → 5-6mi at HMP

### 3. VO2max Development (The Ceiling)
- **Why**: Higher VO2max = more room above LT = faster sustainable pace
- **How**: 800m-1200m reps at 5K pace, 1x per week or every 10-14 days
- **Pfitzinger's periodization**: VO2max intervals come later in the cycle (closer to race day)
- **Purpose is supportive** — maintain/improve the aerobic ceiling while focusing on threshold
- **Frequency**: 1 session every 7-14 days

### 4. Endurance / Long Run Development
- **Why**: The half marathon is still 13.1 miles — aerobic endurance is critical
- **Long runs**: 12-16 miles (some coaches go to 18 for advanced runners)
- **Long run types**: Easy, progressive finish, HMP finish, steady sandwich
- **Medium-long runs** (Pfitzinger): 10-13mi midweek at easy pace — unique to Pfitzinger, very effective for half marathon
- **Purpose**: Builds capillary density, mitochondrial density, fat oxidation

### 5. Running Economy & Speed
- **Why**: Better economy = less oxygen cost at HMP
- **How**: Strides 2-3x/week, occasional 200-400m reps, hill sprints
- **Less emphasis than 5K/10K** — economy gains come mostly from mileage at this distance

## Periodization for Half Marathon (10-14 week cycle)

### Phase 1: Base / Endurance (3-4 weeks)
- Build mileage, easy runs + strides
- Long runs at easy effort (10-13mi)
- Introduce 1 threshold session per week (20-25min tempo)
- Optional: Medium-long run midweek (8-10mi easy)
- Purpose: Aerobic foundation, build volume

### Phase 2: Build / Threshold Focus (3-4 weeks)
- Threshold becomes primary: 2x/week (1 continuous tempo, 1 cruise intervals)
- Introduce VO2max intervals (1x per 10-14 days)
- Long runs start including quality: steady finish, progressive
- Medium-long runs continue (10-13mi)
- Build toward 2x per week of quality threshold
- Purpose: Raise LT aggressively

### Phase 3: Specific / Peak (3-4 weeks)
- HMP work in long runs (3-6mi at HMP in final third)
- Threshold maintained (1-2x/week)
- VO2max 1x per 10-14 days
- Long runs peak at 14-16mi with HMP segments
- Continuous HMP runs (5-8mi) as a quality session
- Mileage holds or slightly decreases
- Purpose: Race-specific sharpening

### Phase 4: Taper (10-14 days)
- **Volume reduction: 40-60%** of peak mileage — progressive reduction
- **Maintain intensity** — shorter quality sessions at race pace and faster
- **Maintain frequency** — don't cut days, shorten each session
- **Research**: 8-14 day taper produced up to 5.6% performance boost
- Final quality session 4-5 days before race
- Last 3 days: easy jog + strides or rest

## Workout Library: Experienced Runners (45+ mpw)

> Paces: HMP = half marathon goal pace, TP = threshold pace (~10-15s/mi slower than HMP for fast runners,
> nearly identical for intermediate), IP = 5K/VO2max pace, SP = strides/rep pace

### 1. 3x2mi at TP (Staple Threshold)
- **Rest**: 90sec-2min jog
- **Total hard**: 6mi at threshold
- **Purpose**: The bread-and-butter threshold workout. Raises LT — the primary limiter for half marathon.
- **Progression**: 2x2mi → 3x2mi → 2x3mi → 4x2mi over the cycle.
- **When**: All phases. The most important workout for half marathon training.

### 2. 2x3mi at TP (Extended Threshold)
- **Rest**: 2min jog or 0.5mi float
- **Total hard**: 6mi at threshold in longer chunks
- **Purpose**: Builds ability to hold threshold for longer continuous periods. More specific to half marathon than shorter reps.
- **When**: Build/peak phases.

### 3. 4x2mi at TP (Peak Threshold Volume)
- **Rest**: 90sec jog
- **Total hard**: 8mi at threshold — massive threshold stimulus
- **Purpose**: Peak volume of threshold work. 8 miles at or near HMP builds race-specific endurance.
- **When**: Peak phase. Only prescribe at 50+ mpw.

### 4. HMP Continuous Run — 5mi
- **Rest**: None (continuous)
- **Pacing**: Exact half marathon goal pace
- **Purpose**: Practice holding goal pace for an extended period. Controlled — can push if feeling good.
- **When**: Build/peak phases. The half marathon equivalent of the marathon MP run.

### 5. HMP Continuous Run — 7mi
- **Rest**: None (continuous)
- **Pacing**: Exact half marathon goal pace
- **Purpose**: Extended race-pace simulation. 7 miles at HMP is more than half the race distance.
- **When**: Peak phase. Only for experienced runners at 50+ mpw.

### 6. 6x1K at IP (VO2max Session)
- **Rest**: 90sec jog
- **Total hard**: ~3.7mi at VO2max pace
- **Purpose**: Maintains/improves VO2max ceiling. Supportive, not primary.
- **When**: Build phase, then every 10-14 days in peak phase.

### 7. 8x800m at IP (VO2max Volume)
- **Rest**: 90sec jog
- **Total hard**: 4mi at VO2max pace
- **Purpose**: Higher volume VO2max. Extra reps build speed endurance.
- **When**: Build phase.

### 8. 4x1mi at TP-10sec (Faster Threshold)
- **Rest**: 2min jog
- **Total hard**: 4mi at slightly faster than threshold
- **Purpose**: Mile reps just below threshold pace. Sharpens the gear between threshold and race pace.
- **When**: Peak phase.

### 9. Threshold + HMP Combo: 2x2mi at TP + 3mi at HMP
- **Rest**: 2min jog between threshold reps, 3min jog before HMP block
- **Total hard**: 7mi of quality (4mi threshold + 3mi HMP)
- **Purpose**: Combines threshold stimulus with race-pace practice. Running HMP on threshold-fatigued legs simulates late-race conditions.
- **When**: Peak phase. The best combo workout for half marathon.

### 10. 5x1.5mi at TP (Cruise Intervals)
- **Rest**: 1min jog (short rest keeps effort elevated)
- **Total hard**: 7.5mi at threshold
- **Purpose**: High-volume cruise intervals. Short rest prevents full recovery — simulates sustained race effort.
- **When**: Build/peak phases.

### 11. Progression Long Run: 14mi (Easy → Steady → HMP)
- **Structure**: 8mi easy, 3mi at steady, 3mi at HMP
- **Purpose**: Progressive build teaches pacing discipline. Finishing at HMP on tired legs is race-specific.
- **When**: Peak phase. The best long run workout for half marathon.

### 12. HMP Finish Long Run: 14-16mi
- **Structure**: 10-12mi easy, 4mi at HMP
- **Purpose**: Race simulation — running HMP after significant easy mileage mimics race conditions.
- **When**: Peak phase. Every 2-3 weeks.

### 13. Steady Sandwich Long Run: 14mi
- **Structure**: 4mi easy, 5mi at steady, 2mi easy, 3mi at HMP
- **Purpose**: Mixed effort with HMP finish. The steady block primes the legs for the quality finish.
- **When**: Peak phase.

### 14. 6x1200m at IP + 2mi at TP (Combo VO2max/Threshold)
- **Rest**: 90sec between 1200s, 3min before threshold block
- **Total hard**: ~4.5mi VO2max + 2mi threshold
- **Purpose**: VO2max stimulus with threshold finish. Running threshold on VO2max-fatigued legs.
- **When**: Build phase.

## Workout Library: Beginner/Intermediate Runners (20-45 mpw)

> Focus: Build aerobic base, develop threshold, introduce HMP gradually.
> Beginners: 1 quality session per week for first 4 weeks, then 2.

### 1. 2x10min at TP (Intro Threshold)
- **Rest**: 3min jog
- **Total hard**: 20min at threshold
- **Purpose**: Introduction to sustained hard effort. The starting point for half marathon training.
- **When**: Build phase.

### 2. 20min Continuous Tempo
- **Rest**: None
- **Total hard**: 20min at threshold
- **Purpose**: Continuous threshold effort. Builds lactate clearance and mental toughness.
- **When**: Build phase. Progress from 15min → 20min → 25min → 30min.

### 3. 4x800m at IP (Intro VO2max)
- **Rest**: 2min jog
- **Total hard**: 2mi at VO2max pace
- **Purpose**: Introduction to fast running. Manageable volume, generous rest.
- **When**: Build phase.

### 4. 2x2mi at TP (Standard Threshold)
- **Rest**: 2min jog
- **Total hard**: 4mi at threshold
- **Purpose**: The foundational threshold workout. Simple and effective.
- **When**: Build phase. Progress to 3x2mi.

### 5. 3x1mi at HMP (Race Pace Introduction)
- **Rest**: 2min jog
- **Total hard**: 3mi at half marathon pace
- **Purpose**: Learn what HMP feels like in manageable doses.
- **When**: Build/peak phase.

### 6. Fartlek: 6x3min Hard / 2min Easy
- **Rest**: 2min easy jog
- **Pacing**: Hard segments at threshold-to-HMP effort
- **Total hard**: 18min of quality
- **Purpose**: Sustained effort fartlek. Builds threshold fitness without the rigidity of track intervals.
- **When**: Base/build phase.

### 7. 25min Tempo + Strides
- **Structure**: 25min continuous at TP, then 4x20sec strides at mile effort
- **Purpose**: Threshold stimulus with neuromuscular speed kicker.
- **When**: Build phase.

### 8. 5x1K at TP (Threshold Kilometers)
- **Rest**: 90sec jog
- **Total hard**: ~3mi at threshold
- **Purpose**: Kilometer-based threshold. Short enough reps to maintain quality. Good for runners who find continuous tempos daunting.
- **When**: Build phase.

### 9. 6x800m at IP (Standard VO2max)
- **Rest**: 2min jog
- **Total hard**: 3mi at VO2max pace
- **Purpose**: The standard VO2max session for intermediate runners.
- **When**: Build/peak phase.

### 10. HMP Finish Long Run: 10-12mi
- **Structure**: 8-10mi easy, 2mi at HMP
- **Purpose**: Introduction to quality long runs. Just 2mi at HMP — enough to practice race pace without overdoing it.
- **When**: Peak phase. Progress from 2mi HMP to 3mi.

### 11. Progressive Long Run: 10-12mi
- **Structure**: 7-9mi easy, last 2-3mi descending to steady pace (not threshold)
- **Purpose**: Teaches finishing strong without the stress of a full workout. Controlled quality.
- **When**: Build phase. Every 2-3 weeks.

### 12. Easy Long Run with Pickups
- **Structure**: 10-12mi easy with 6x30sec pickups at 10K effort scattered through middle miles
- **Purpose**: Adds neuromuscular stimulus without making it a workout.
- **When**: Base and build phases.

## Pace Calculation Guidelines for Plan Engine

### From Goal Half Marathon Time
- **Race Pace (HMP)**: goal_time / 13.1094 (sec/mi)
- **Interval Pace (IP)**: HMP * 0.88 (~12% faster — approximates 5K pace)
- **Threshold Pace (TP)**: HMP * 0.97 (~3% faster than HMP for fast runners, very close to HMP)
- **Easy Pace**: HMP * 1.30 to HMP * 1.50 (30-50% slower)
- **Steady Pace**: TP + 20-25sec (between easy and threshold)
- **Strides Pace (SP)**: HMP * 0.78 (~mile race effort)
- **Long Run Pace**: HMP * 1.35 (easy, conversational)

### Important Note on TP vs HMP
For faster runners (sub-1:30), threshold pace and HMP are very close — within 5-10 sec/mi.
For intermediate runners (1:45-2:00), threshold pace is noticeably faster than HMP (~15-20 sec/mi).
The plan engine should handle both cases correctly.

### Current Fitness Progression
- Same blending pattern as other distances
- Threshold progression is critical — most of the improvement comes from raising LT
- HMP work introduced in second half of cycle (after 4-6 weeks of base/build)

### Pace Rounding
- All paces rounded to nearest 5 seconds
- Interval paces shown as range (±5sec) for flexibility

## Special Half Marathon Training Concepts

### Medium-Long Runs (Pfitzinger's Key Innovation)
- 10-13mi midweek at easy pace
- NOT a workout — purely aerobic, conversational
- Unique to Pfitzinger's approach — most coaches don't prescribe these
- Adds significant aerobic volume without quality-session fatigue
- Only for runners at 50+ mpw who can handle the extra distance midweek
- Purpose: Builds endurance without adding another hard day

### Steady-State Runs
- Continuous runs at slightly slower than threshold (within ~15-20 sec/mi of TP)
- "Especially beneficial for the half marathon" — directly targets the energy system used in racing
- Can be 30-50 minutes continuous
- Less stressful than threshold work, more beneficial than easy running
- Good bridge between easy days and quality days

### The HMP-Threshold Overlap
- For fast runners, HMP and threshold pace converge
- This means threshold workouts ARE race-pace workouts
- For these runners, the key is extending the DURATION at threshold, not finding a different pace
- 40min continuous tempo at threshold = essentially a half marathon pace run

### Half Marathon Fueling in Training
- Practice fueling in long runs (gel at mile 5-6, another at mile 9-10)
- The half marathon is the shortest race where fueling can make a measurable difference
- Not critical like marathon, but can prevent the last 2-3 mile fade
